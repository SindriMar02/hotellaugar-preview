Breakfast

A buffet in the restaurant, included for hotel guests and open to visitors. Fresh bread and pastries, skyr, eggs and herring, and bean-to-cup coffee. Gluten free on request. Ask the evening before for a lunch pack to take on the road.

Dinner

Our Icelandic chef cooks with local products: Arctic char, Icelandic lamb, soups and salads from the valley, and skyr for dessert. The dining room looks out at the pond. A reservation is recommended, and outside guests are welcome.

  • Arctic charfrom the lakes nearby
  • Icelandic lambwith beetroot and jus
  • Soup of the daywith bread
  • Saladsfrom the kitchen garden season
  • Skyramisu and chocolate cakedessert

The menu changes with what arrives. Tell us about allergies when you book a table.

Lounge open around the clock · bar from 16:00 · happy hour 16:00 to 18:00

Lounge, barand terrace

The lounge is open day and night for reading and games. Drinks are served from four in the afternoon, with happy hour until six, and there is usually a cake on the counter. The sun terrace faces Reykjadalur, with the river audible below, and there is a sitting area at the edge of the pond.

Not staying?Come for dinner.

Outside guests are welcome for breakfast and dinner. Call ahead for a table, especially in July.
