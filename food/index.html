Dinner without leaving the valley

After a day exploring North Iceland, sometimes the best plan is to come home. Join us for dinner at Hotel Laugar, enjoy a drink in the lounge, or take your glass outside onto the sun terrace when the Icelandic weather is kind to us. Our kitchen focuses on fresh, local ingredients and Icelandic favourites, including dishes such as Arctic char and Icelandic lamb.

A reservation is recommended, and outside guests are welcome for breakfast and dinner. The menu changes with what arrives; tell us about allergies when you book a table.

Start your Icelandic day well

Breakfast is always included at Hotel Laugar. Our generous breakfast buffet gives you everything you need before heading out into North Iceland, whether you’re setting off early for the Diamond Circle or simply enjoying a slower morning in Reykjadalur.
