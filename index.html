Your summer base in North Iceland

Peaceful days. Big landscapes. A warm welcome.

In the heart of Reykjadalur, Hotel Laugar is a friendly summer hotel and an ideal base for exploring North Iceland — from Goðafoss and Lake Mývatn to Húsavík, the Diamond Circle and the landscapes beyond.

A hotel for the Icelandic summer

Hotel Laugar has a story of its own. During the school year, our buildings are home to a boarding school. When the Icelandic summer arrives and the students leave for their holidays, we open the doors as Hotel Laugar.

For a few precious months, the classrooms become quiet, the surrounding countryside turns wonderfully green, and we welcome travellers from around the world.

Good to know

All questions
When is reception open?

From 07:00 to 12:00 and from 16:00 to 22:00, in the main building. Check-in is from 16:00 to 22:00 and check-out by 11:00. Arriving after 22:00? Call us that day and we tell you how to check in late.

How do I get to my room?

Drive up to the main building, past the swimming pool to the flags, and check in first. Tröllasteinn is right beside the car park with a lift. Álfasteinn and Fjall are a short drive, or a minute’s walk by path, and a porter is available on request.

Is the hotel wheelchair accessible?

Tröllasteinn has a lift, direct access from the car park and two wheelchair accessible rooms. The restaurant is also accessible, by the outdoor path. Álfasteinn and Fjall are reached by stairs. If you cannot climb stairs, please let us know in advance, as not every room is reached by a lift.

What are the cancellation terms?

You choose when you book: a non-refundable rate paid now, or a refundable rate secured with your card details, verified with 3D Secure. The exact terms for each are shown before you pay.

By car from Akureyri
About 50 km, 45 minutes east on the ring road, through the Vaðlaheiðargöng tunnel and past Goðafoss. The tunnel toll is paid online after you drive through.
From Mývatn
30 km west on the ring road, about half an hour. The hotel is in the village, on road 846, 1 km off the ring road.
By air
Reykjavík to Akureyri is about 45 minutes in the air. Collect a hire car at the airport and you are at Laugar in about 45 minutes more.
When you arrive
Drive up past the swimming pool to the flags and check in at the main building by the pond. Reception is open 07:00 to 12:00 and 16:00 to 22:00; arriving later, call us that day.
