Hótel Laugar, Reykjadalur

A peaceful summer base for discovering North Iceland. Three white houses by a lake in Reykjadalur, between Goðafoss and Mývatn.

Open 31 May to 22 August 2027 Book direct

Hótel Laugar is a boarding school for most of the year. In the summer holidays it becomes a 57-room hotel in the quiet of Reykjadalur: white houses with red roofs, a pond between them, the river audible from the terrace, and the Diamond Circle beginning at the end of the drive.

White houses,red roofs, still water.

The buildings belong to the school at Laugar. In the summer holidays the rooms are made up, the restaurant opens and the flags go up. You check in at the main building by the pond, then drive or walk to your house: Tröllasteinn beside the car park, Álfasteinn round the pool, Fjall up the hill with the view down the valley.

Three houses,one reception.

Ample parking at every building, wifi in all of them, and a free porter on request for the two houses away from the car park.

  1. Reception and restaurantBy the pondCheck in here, collect your key cards. Restaurant, lounge, bar and the sun terrace facing the valley.
  2. Tröllasteinn35 roomsStraight from the car park, with a lift. Two wheelchair-accessible rooms, a step-free path to dinner.
  3. Álfasteinn7 roomsA short drive round the swimming pool, own parking, first floor by stairs. The quietest house.
  4. Fjall15 roomsUp the hill with the best view, a guest lounge over the valley, the triple room, floors renovated this year.

57 rooms,made up plainly.

Doubles that become twins, one natural triple, and extra beds for a third guest on request. Every room has its own bathroom, a kettle with coffee and tea, and a window to the valley or the grounds.

The long day at Laugar

Breakfast at seven, cake at four, dinner at six. In June the sun barely sets.

07:00 to 10:00

Breakfast

A buffet with breads and pastries, skyr, eggs and herring, cold cuts and cheeses, fruit and vegetables, bean-to-cup coffee and about ten teas. Gluten free on request.

11:00

Out the door

Check-out is at eleven. Order a lunch pack the evening before and take the day to Mývatn, Húsavík or the waterfalls.

16:00 to 18:00

Check-in, then happy hour

Rooms are ready from four. The lounge is open around the clock, drinks are served from four, and happy hour runs until six. There is usually cake.

18:00 to 21:00

Dinner

Our Icelandic chef cooks with local produce: Arctic char, lamb, soup, salads, and a skyr dessert. Reservation recommended. Outside guests are welcome.

Late

The sun does not really set

In June the light stays all night. The terrace faces the valley, the river is audible, and the lounge is open whenever you come back.

In the middle ofthe Diamond Circle.

Laugar sits at the crossroads of North East Iceland. Stay two to four nights and see the whole circle from one bed. Distances by road from the hotel.

  1. Goðafoss15 kmThe waterfall of the gods, a quarter of an hour west.
  2. Mývatn30 kmCraters, lava fields and the Nature Baths, half an hour east.
  3. Húsavík40 kmWhale watching from the harbour, forty minutes north.
  4. Akureyri60 kmThe capital of the north and its airport, an hour west.
  5. Dettifoss and Ásbyrgi100 kmThe big falls and the canyon, a full day east and back for dinner.

Good to know

All questions
Is Hótel Laugar open all year?

No. The buildings are a school for most of the year. The hotel is open in the summer holidays, in 2027 from 31 May to 22 August.

When is reception open?

From 07:00 to 12:00 and from 16:00 to 22:00, in the main building. Check-in is from 16:00 and check-out by 11:00.

How do I get to my room?

Check in at the main building first. Tröllasteinn is right beside the car park with a lift. Álfasteinn and Fjall are a short drive, or a minute’s walk by path, and a porter is available on request.

Is there a swimming pool?

The village pool, Sundlaugin Laugum, is next door with a big pool, two hot tubs, a children’s pool, a cold tub and a sauna. Entry is paid at the pool. Towels are complimentary from reception.

Can I have dinner without staying at the hotel?

Yes. Outside guests are welcome for breakfast and dinner. Dinner is served from 18:00 to 21:00 and a reservation is recommended.

Is there wifi and parking?

Both are free. There is ample parking at every building, and wifi in all buildings. The signal can drop while you walk between them.
