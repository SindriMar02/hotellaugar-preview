Privacy

This website belongs to Hótel Laugar ehf., Austurhlíðarvegur, 650 Laugar, Iceland (kt. 610616-0460). It is a plain website: it shows you the hotel and lets you start a booking. Here is what it does and does not do with your data.

No cookies, no analytics

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. One small value may be kept in your browser’s session storage for the page-turn animation; it holds no personal data and disappears when the tab closes.

Booking

When you choose dates and press "See rooms and rates", you leave this site for the hotel’s booking page run by Godo (property.godo.is). Your dates and number of guests are passed in the address of that page. Everything you enter there, including payment, is handled by Godo and its payment provider under their own terms, which are shown before you pay. Nothing from the booking is stored on this site.

Email, phone and WhatsApp

If you write to hotellaugar@hotellaugar.is, call +354 466 4009 or message +354 884 3259 on WhatsApp, the hotel keeps your message for as long as it is needed to answer you and to manage your stay. Email is hosted by the hotel’s email provider.

Third parties on this site

Photographs, fonts and scripts are served from this site’s own address. Links to Villa North, Akureyri Lodge, Kip, Snow Dogs Iceland, Google Maps, Instagram and WhatsApp open those services, which have their own privacy terms.

Your rights

You can ask the hotel what it holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to hotellaugar@hotellaugar.is. The Icelandic Data Protection Authority, Persónuvernd, handles complaints.
