Explore North Iceland

Stay in Reykjadalur, between Akureyri, Húsavík and Mývatn. Every stop on the Diamond Circle is a day trip from here, and you come home to the same room each night.

Around us, by car

Laugar sits on the Diamond Circle, the driving route linking Goðafoss, Mývatn, Húsavík, Ásbyrgi and Dettifoss. Do it in one long summer day, or spread it over several. Plan the Diamond Circle

Within half an hour

15 km · 15 minAdded

Goðafoss

The waterfall of the gods. Legend says Iceland’s law speaker threw the old idols into it in the year 1000. It lies right by the ring road: an hour’s stop, or half a day.

Pairs with Akureyri

Goðafoss in detail

30 km · 30 minAdded

Lake Mývatn

Craters formed by steam explosions, the lava formations of Dimmuborgir and the steaming vents at Hverir, then the geothermal baths above the lake. Plan a full day. The lake is named for its midges, which gather on still days, so a head net helps.

A full day

40 km · 40 minAdded

Húsavík

A harbour town on Skjálfandi bay and the departure point for whale watching tours. Above the harbour, the GeoSea sea baths look out over the bay.

Tours run through the summer

Húsavík in detail

50 km · 45 minAdded

Akureyri

Known as the capital of the north, through the Vaðlaheiði tunnel. Visit the botanical garden, climb the steps to the church or swim at the town pool. The airport serves domestic flights.

Stop at Goðafoss on the way

Akureyri in detail

A full day out

90 to 100 km · 1 h 20 to 1 h 30Added

Dettifoss and Ásbyrgi

Dettifoss, in Vatnajökull National Park, is the most powerful waterfall in Europe. Ásbyrgi is a horseshoe canyon that legend calls the hoofprint of Odin’s horse, with woodland walks and a still pond under the cliffs.

Together, a full day. Road 862 to the west bank of Dettifoss is paved all the way.

Dettifoss and Ásbyrgi in detail

The village, on foot

Laugar sits beside the Reykjadalsá river in the valley of Reykjadalur. The pool, a round of golf and the river path are all a short walk from the hotel. The valley in detail

  • The poolSundlaugin Laugum is a minute’s walk away: a 25 metre outdoor pool, two hot tubs, a children’s pool, a cold tub, a sauna and an outdoor shower. Open 10:00 to 21:00 in summer. Admission is paid at the pool, and the gym upstairs costs extra. Towels are free at hotel reception.
  • GolfA golf course in Laugar, paid separately.
  • WalksThe Reykjadalsá river is two minutes on foot from the hotel. Follow the path along the river from the village.

Before you head out

Check the weather, road conditions and SafeTravel alerts each morning before you set off. Weather can change the day, so keep your plans flexible.

Nights darken again in late August, and the northern lights can appear on clear nights. A sighting is never guaranteed.
