Explore North Iceland

Laugar is at the crossroads of the north east. Every stop on the Diamond Circle is a day trip from the same bed.

By road from the hotel

Distances are driving distances from Laugar. Two nights covers Goðafoss and Mývatn. A third adds Húsavík and the whales, a fourth the big falls and Ásbyrgi.

Goðafoss 15 km

The waterfall of the gods on the Skjálfandafljót, where the law speaker is said to have thrown the old idols in the year 1000. A quarter of an hour west along the ring road, and best early or late when the coaches have gone.

Mývatn 30 km

Pseudo craters, the lava of Dimmuborgir, the steam at Hverir and the Nature Baths. Half an hour east. The midges that give the lake its name are worst on still days; a head net from the petrol station is money well spent.

Húsavík 40 km

The whale watching harbour of Iceland, forty minutes north on Skjálfandi bay. Humpbacks most days in summer, blue whales some years. Book the boat the day before and allow three hours.

Akureyri 60 km

The capital of the north, an hour west through the Vaðlaheiði tunnel: the botanical garden, the church on the steps, the swimming pool, and the airport for domestic flights.

Dettifoss 100 km

The most powerful waterfall in Europe, in Vatnajökull National Park. About an hour and a half east. The west bank road is paved; check conditions on road.is before you go.

Ásbyrgi 100 km

The horseshoe canyon, said to be the hoofprint of Óðinn’s horse. Woods, a still pond under the cliffs, and short walks. Combine with Dettifoss for a full day and come back for dinner.

At the door

Not everything needs the car.

  • The pool, Sundlaugin Laugum, a minute’s walk: big pool, two hot tubs, children’s pool, cold tub, sauna. Paid at the pool, towels from reception.
  • Golf: a six hole course right outside the hotel, paid separately.
  • Horse riding nearby, paid separately. Ask at reception for the day’s options.
  • Walks along the Reykjadalsá river and up the hill behind Fjall for the valley view.
  • Tours from Húsavík and Mývatn can be booked at reception.
