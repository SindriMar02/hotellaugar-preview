Explore North Iceland

Reykjadalur, between Akureyri, Húsavík and Mývatn. Every stop on the Diamond Circle is a day trip from the same bed.

Reykjadalur, not merely near Mývatn

The valley of the Reykjadalsá river, with Laugar in the middle of it: the school, the village pool, the farms and the road north to Húsavík. Goðafoss is a quarter of an hour west on the ring road, Mývatn half an hour east. Two nights covers both. A third adds Húsavík and the whales, a fourth the big falls and Ásbyrgi.

By road from the hotel

Goðafoss 15 km · 15 min

The waterfall of the gods on the Skjálfandafljót, where the law speaker is said to have thrown the old idols in the year 1000. West along the ring road; best early or late when the coaches have gone. Half a day, or an hour on the way to Akureyri.

Day trip: yesCombine with: Akureyri

Lake Mývatn 30 km · 30 min

Pseudo craters, the lava of Dimmuborgir, the steam at Hverir and the Nature Baths. A full day, and the midges that give the lake its name are worst on still days; a head net from the petrol station is money well spent.

Day trip: yesCombine with: Dettifoss

Húsavík 40 km · 40 min

The whale watching capital of Europe, north on Skjálfandi bay, with tours from the harbour and the sea baths above the town.

Day trip: yesBest: June to August

Akureyri 60 km · 1 h

The capital of the north, west through the Vaðlaheiði tunnel: the botanical garden, the church on the steps, the pool, and the airport for domestic flights.

Day trip: yesCombine with: Goðafoss

Dettifoss 100 km · 1 h 30

The most powerful waterfall in Europe, in Vatnajökull National Park. The west bank road is paved; check road.is before you go.

Day trip: yesCombine with: Ásbyrgi

Ásbyrgi 100 km · 1 h 30

The horseshoe canyon, said to be the hoofprint of Óðinn’s horse. Woods, a still pond under the cliffs, short walks. With Dettifoss it is a full day, and you are back for dinner.

Day trip: yesCombine with: Dettifoss

The Diamond Circle one day, or three

Goðafoss, Mývatn, Dettifoss, Ásbyrgi and Húsavík make the circle, and Laugar is inside it. Drive it in one long summer day, or take a piece each day and come home for dinner.

Base: 2 to 4 nights
