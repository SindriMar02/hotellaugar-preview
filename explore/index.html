Explore North Iceland

Stay in Reykjadalur, between Akureyri, Húsavík and Mývatn, and visit every stop on the Diamond Circle on day trips from the same base.

Reykjadalur, the valley of Laugar

Laugar is in Reykjadalur, by the Reykjadalsá river, with the school, the village pool, the farms and the road north to Húsavík. Stay two nights for Goðafoss, 15 minutes west on the ring road, and Mývatn, half an hour east. With three, add Húsavík and the whales. With four, add Dettifoss and Ásbyrgi.

By road from the hotel

Laugar is on the Diamond Circle: Goðafoss, Mývatn, Dettifoss, Ásbyrgi and Húsavík. Drive it in one long summer day, or explore it over several days. Plan the Diamond Circle

  1. Goðafoss15 km · 15 min

    The waterfall of the gods on the Skjálfandafljót, where the law speaker is said to have thrown the old idols in the year 1000. West along the ring road; best early in the morning or in the evening. Half a day, or an hour on the way to Akureyri.Pairs with Akureyri · Goðafoss in detail

  2. Lake Mývatn30 km · 30 min

    Pseudo craters, the lava of Dimmuborgir, the steam at Hverir and the geothermal baths above the lake. A full day, and the midges that give the lake its name are worst on still days; a head net from the petrol station is money well spent.Pairs with Dettifoss · Mývatn in detail

  3. Húsavík40 km · 40 min

    The whale watching capital of Europe, north on Skjálfandi bay, with tours from the harbour and the sea baths above the town.Best June to August · Húsavík in detail

  4. Akureyri50 km · 45 min

    The capital of the north, west through the Vaðlaheiði tunnel: the botanical garden, the church on the steps, the pool, and the airport for domestic flights.Pairs with Goðafoss · Akureyri in detail

  5. Dettifoss90 km · 1 h 20

    The most powerful waterfall in Europe, in Vatnajökull National Park. Road 862 on the west bank is paved all the way; check road.is before you go.Pairs with Ásbyrgi · Dettifoss and Ásbyrgi in detail

  6. Ásbyrgi100 km · 1 h 30

    The horseshoe canyon, said to be the hoofprint of Óðinn’s horse. Woods, a still pond under the cliffs, short walks. With Dettifoss it is a full day.Pairs with Dettifoss
