Rooms & Stay

57 rooms in three white houses. Standard double rooms that can all be set up as twin, single rooms, one natural triple, every room with its own bathroom.

In every room

The rooms are plain, clean and warm. A double bed that splits into twins on request, an extra bed for a third guest at extra cost, baby cribs on request, and any room can be booked for one guest. There are no TVs: for most of the year the houses are a school.

  • Private bathroom
  • Linen, towels and a bathmat
  • Hairdryer
  • Soap, shampoo and body wash
  • Kettle with coffee and tea
  • Adjustable heating
  • Free wifi
  • Additional towels for the pool, on request

Three houses, named for the hidden folk

Trolls, elves and a ghost: the guest houses carry the names of stones in the valley. Fjall is really Draugasteinn, the ghost stone, but nobody wanted to frighten the guests. Check in at the main building by the pond, then drive or walk to your house.

Reception and restaurant are in the main building by the pond; there are no rooms there.Tröllasteinn 301 to 335 · Álfasteinn 401 to 407 · Fjall 101 to 115

Good to know

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 16:00 to 22:00, check-out by 11:00. Reception is open 07:00 to 12:00 and 16:00 to 22:00. Arriving after 22:00? Call us that day and we tell you how to check in late. Early check-in or late check-out when a room is free, for guests who book direct.

Laundry

Laundry service at a cost. For a one night stay we can wash what goes in the dryer. From two nights, clothes can also air dry.

Wifi

Free in all buildings. The signal can drop while you walk between the houses.

Drinking water

Tap water in Iceland is clean and free, straight from underground springs. Drink it from the tap.

Stairs and accessibility

Tröllasteinn has a lift and two wheelchair accessible rooms. Álfasteinn and Fjall are reached by stairs. If you cannot climb stairs, please let us know in advance, as not every room is reached by a lift.

Families

Extra beds for a third guest on request, at extra cost, and baby cribs on request. One natural triple room in Fjall. The children’s pool is next door at Sundlaugin Laugum.
