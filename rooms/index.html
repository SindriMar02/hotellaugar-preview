Rooms

Rooms and stay

57 rooms in three white houses. Doubles that become twins, one natural triple, every room with its own bathroom.

Three houses, named forthe hidden folk.

Trolls, elves and ghosts: the guest houses carry the names of the stones in the valley. Fjall is really Draugasteinn, the ghost stone, but nobody wanted to frighten the guests.

Tröllasteinn 35 rooms

The main guest building, with direct access from the car park and a lift to every floor. The outdoor path to the restaurant is step free.

  • Ground floor7 rooms, facing the car park
  • First and second floor14 rooms each
  • Wheelchair accessible2 rooms
  • Liftyes

Álfasteinn 7 rooms

A short drive round the swimming pool, with its own parking. The quietest house, with seven rooms on the first floor.

  • First floor7 rooms
  • Liftno, stairs
  • Porterfree, on request
  • To receptiona minute by path

Fjall 15 rooms

A short drive up the hill and the best view on the property. The ground floor sits partly in the hillside and the triple room is here. Floors renovated this year.

  • First floor3 rooms and the guest lounge
  • Ground floor7 rooms, incl. the triple
  • Second floor5 rooms, two staircases
  • Porterfree, on request

In every room

The rooms are plain, clean and warm. A double bed that splits into twins on request, and an extra bed for a third guest at extra cost.

  • Private bathroom with shower
  • Linen, towels and a bathmat
  • Hairdryer
  • Soap, shampoo and body wash
  • Kettle with coffee and tea
  • Adjustable heating
  • Free wifi
  • Window to the valley or the grounds

Good to know

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 16:00, check-out by 11:00. Reception is open 07:00 to 12:00 and 16:00 to 22:00. Early check-in or late check-out when a room is free, for guests who book direct.

Laundry

For a one night stay we can wash what goes in the dryer. From two nights, clothes can also air dry.

Wifi

Free in all buildings. The signal can drop while you walk between the houses.

Families

Extra beds for a third guest on request, at extra cost. One natural triple room in Fjall. The children’s pool is next door at Sundlaugin Laugum.

Check-out has to be after check-in.
