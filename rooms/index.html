Rooms & Stay

57 rooms in three white houses. Standard double rooms that can all be set up as twin, single rooms, one natural triple, every room with its own bathroom.

The room, plainly

Plain, clean and warm. A double bed that splits into twins on request, and any room can be booked for one guest. There are no TVs: for most of the year the houses are a school.

  1. One or twoEvery room, double or twin.
  2. ThreeAn extra bed at extra cost, or the one natural triple in Fjall.
  3. With a babyBaby cribs on request.

Wheelchair accessible: two rooms in Tröllasteinn, with a lift, direct access from the car park and a step-free path to the restaurant. Tell us in advance.

In the room
  • Private bathroom
  • Linen, towels and a bathmat
  • Hairdryer
  • Soap, shampoo and body wash
  • Kettle with coffee and tea
  • Adjustable heating
  • Free wifi
  • Towels for the pool, on request

Three houses, named for the hidden folk

A troll, an elf and a ghost: the guest houses are named for the hidden folk. Fjall is really Draugasteinn, the ghost stone, but nobody wanted to frighten the guests.

Check in at the main building by the pond, then drive or walk to your house. Tell us which house you prefer when you book.

Good to know

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 16:00 to 22:00, check-out by 11:00. Reception is open 07:00 to 12:00 and 16:00 to 22:00. Arriving after 22:00? Call us that day and we tell you how to check in late. Early check-in or late check-out when a room is free, for guests who book direct.

Laundry

Laundry service at a cost. For a one night stay we can wash what goes in the dryer. From two nights, clothes can also air dry.

Wifi

Free in all buildings. The signal can drop while you walk between the houses.

Drinking water

Cold tap water in Iceland is clean and free. Drink it from the tap.

Families

Extra beds for a third guest on request, at extra cost, and baby cribs on request. One natural triple room in Fjall. The children’s pool is next door at Sundlaugin Laugum.

The swimming pool

The village pool is a minute’s walk. Towels are complimentary from reception; admission is paid at the pool.
