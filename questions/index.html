The hotel

Is Hótel Laugar open all year?

No. The buildings are a school for most of the year. The hotel is open in the summer holidays, in 2027 from 31 May to 22 August.

When are check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 16:00, check-out by 11:00. Early check-in or late check-out when a room is free, for guests who book direct.

When is reception open?

From 07:00 to 12:00 and from 16:00 to 22:00, in the main building.

How do I get to my room?

Drive to the main building, check in and collect your key cards, then drive or walk to your house. Tröllasteinn is beside the car park with a lift. Álfasteinn is a short drive round the pool, Fjall a short drive up the hill. Both are a minute’s walk by path, and a porter is available free on request.

Is the hotel wheelchair accessible?

Tröllasteinn has a lift, direct access from the car park and two wheelchair accessible rooms. The restaurant is also accessible, by the outdoor path. Álfasteinn and Fjall are reached by stairs.

Is there parking?

Yes, free and ample, at every building.

Is there wifi?

Free in all buildings. The signal can drop while you walk between them.

Can I do laundry?

For a one night stay we can wash what goes in the dryer. From two nights, clothes can also air dry.

Rooms

What kinds of room are there?

57 rooms: one natural triple, and doubles that can be made up as twins. An extra bed for a third guest can be added on request, at extra cost.

What is in the room?

A private bathroom, linen, towels and a bathmat, hairdryer, soap, shampoo and body wash, a kettle with coffee and tea, and adjustable heating.

Which house should I choose?

Tröllasteinn if you want the lift, the car park at the door and the shortest walk to dinner. Álfasteinn for quiet. Fjall for the view and the guest lounge. Rooms are assigned by the hotel; tell us your preference when you book.

Food and drink

When is breakfast?

A buffet from 07:00 to 10:00 in the restaurant. Gluten free on request.

When is dinner, and do I need to book?

Dinner is served from 18:00 to 21:00. A reservation is recommended, and outside guests are welcome.

Is there a bar?

Drinks are served in the lounge from 16:00, with happy hour from 16:00 to 18:00. The lounge itself is open around the clock.

Can I get a packed lunch?

Yes, on request. Order the evening before.

Around

Is there a swimming pool?

The village pool, Sundlaugin Laugum, is a minute’s walk: a big outdoor pool, two hot tubs, a children’s pool, a cold tub and a sauna, with a paid gym upstairs. Entry is paid at the pool. Towels are complimentary from reception.

How far are Goðafoss, Mývatn and Húsavík?

Goðafoss 15 km, Lake Mývatn 30 km, Húsavík 40 km, Akureyri 60 km, Dettifoss and Ásbyrgi about 100 km, all by road.

How do I find the hotel?

Hótel Laugar, Austurhlíðarvegur, 650 Laugar, in Reykjadalur, just off the ring road between Akureyri and Mývatn. Open in Google Maps.

Booking

Why book direct?

Our best available rate, and early check-in or late check-out when a room is free.

What are the cancellation terms?

You choose when you book: a non-refundable rate paid now, or a refundable rate held on your card. The exact terms for each are shown before you pay.

Do you take groups?

Yes. Write to hotellaugar@hotellaugar.is with dates and numbers.
